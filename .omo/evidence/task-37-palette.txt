T37 — Unified accent palette across desktop + landing
=====================================================

DECISION: `#1d4ed8` (Linear blue) is the canonical OpenLinear brand accent.
          Runtime-themable in desktop-ui via the in-app accent picker.

ACTIONS TAKEN
-------------
1. apps/landing/app/globals.css
   - Replaced purple/indigo primary (`243 40% 46%`) with canonical blue
     (`224 76% 48%` = `#1d4ed8`) in BOTH `:root` (light) and `.dark` blocks.
   - Updated all CTA-adjacent tokens: --primary, --accent, --ring, --chart-1,
     --sidebar-primary, --sidebar-ring.
   - Demoted dark-mode warm cream (`49 35% 72%`) from primary/accent to
     decorative-only via NEW landing-scoped tokens:
       --landing-warm-cream: #ede8d0;
       --landing-warm-cream-rgb: 237, 232, 208;
   - Added canonical accent tokens (matching desktop-ui defaults):
       --linear-accent: #1d4ed8;
       --linear-accent-hover: #1e40af;

2. apps/desktop-ui/app/globals.css — UNCHANGED (already correct):
       --linear-accent: #1d4ed8;
       --linear-accent-hover: #1e40af;
   These are runtime-overridden by the accent picker.

3. docs/brand.md — NEW. Documents:
   - Canonical accent hex values + when each token applies
   - Runtime themability mechanism (desktop-ui only)
   - Landing-only warm-cream decoration scope (forbidden for CTAs)
   - Per-app surface palette
   - Audit checklist for new UI work
